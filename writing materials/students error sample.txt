The illustration demonstrates the process of electricity production using geothermal energy.
Overall, the linear, man-made process consists off five steps in the sequence, beginning with cold water being pumped down and ending with the transmission of electricity.
Initially, cold water is pumped down through an injection well, reaching a depth of 4500 meters. Next, the cold liquid flows through an the geothermal zone which has hot rocks. By these stones, water then is heated before being transported to a production well. Once contained in the production well, hot water is pumped up the surface with the same depth to condense.
Following this, the hot liquid is rapidly converted to stream by a condenser, prior to being conveyed to a turbine. After powered by stream, the turbine starts rotating, resulting in the operation of the generator to produce electricity. Eventually, electric energy is generated and contributed for different purposes via power lines.

The diagram illustrates the process of producing smoked fish, from catching the fish to selling the final product in shops.
Overall, it is a linear process consisting of several stages, beginning with fishing and ending with distribution to fish shops. Notably, the fish undergo multiple treatments, including cleaning, seasoning and smoking, as well as two freezing stages before being sold.
At the beginning of the process, fish are caught using nets and then transported by boat to the port. After arriving at the harbour, they are frozen to preserve freshness. Following this, the fish are placed in fresh water, after which their heads are cut off and the bodies are opened for further processing.
In the next stage, the prepared fish are soaked in salt water mixed with yellow colouring, which helps to season them and improve their appearance. They are then smoked to enhance flavour. After being smoked, the fish are packed into containers and frozen again at a temperature of around 0 degrees celsius .Next, they are stored in cold storage before being distributed by truck to fish shops, where they are finally sold to consumers.
